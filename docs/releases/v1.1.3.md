# rustyAC v1.1.3

Browser fix: Full mode at a render scale below 100 % shows one whole picture again.

## What's in it

- **Full mode's render scale** ([docs/web_fixes.md](https://github.com/thesupersupersigma/rustyAC/blob/v1.1.3/docs/web_fixes.md), W3):
  at 50 % or 75 % the 3D picture sat in the top-left corner of the canvas, with black or stale
  pieces of older frames around it, whenever the render scale took effect after the drive had
  started (and on a first drive, AC's app bar showed in the middle of the screen). Both had been
  there since v0.48.2 / v0.40.0. The picture now fills the canvas at every scale, set before the
  drive or changed during it; at 100 % nothing changed.
- The audio crackling heard on v1.1.2 under heavy load did not show on an idle PC (the sound's
  feed never ran late); nothing was changed there.
