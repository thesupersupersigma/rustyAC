rustyAC @VERSION@ - how to run
=====================================

rustyAC is a Rust rewrite of Assetto Corsa. It is very experimental: one car on a
track or on an endless flat road, in a debug view.

Source, notes and newer versions: https://github.com/thesupersupersigma/rustyAC


1. What you need
----------------

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with a graphics card that does Direct3D 11.

- Your own Assetto Corsa install. No Assetto Corsa file comes with rustyAC:
  the tracks, the car models and the car data are read from your own game.
  rustyAC finds the game by itself in Steam's usual place
  (C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\assettocorsa) and in every
  other Steam library (it reads Steam's libraryfolders.vdf). If your game is
  somewhere else, say where it is:

      set AC_ROOT=D:\Games\assettocorsa

  Nothing has to be unpacked or copied. A car is named by its folder name under
  content\cars (--car ks_ferrari_f2004, which is also the default). Its data is
  read straight out of content\cars\<car>\data.acd, in memory, the way the game
  does it; a car that has a plain "data" folder instead (the SDK's cars,
  unpacked mods) is read from that. Nothing is written into the game's folder.

  --car also takes a folder: a car's folder, or a folder with the plain data
  files (car.ini, tyres.ini, ...) in it:

      rustyac.exe --car "D:\my cars\my_car\data"

  Not every car drives yet: only cars with double-wishbone suspension all
  round, rear- or front-wheel drive and no hybrid system. Any other car is
  refused with a message that says which system is missing.

- Optional: the Visual C++ 2013 runtime (MSVCR120.dll). Assetto Corsa installs
  it, so you most likely have it. With it rustyAC computes with exactly the
  game's maths; without it Rust's own maths is used.


2. Commands
-----------

Open a console (cmd or PowerShell) in this folder.

    rustyac.exe --track spa --windowed       drive at Spa, in a window
    rustyac.exe --track spa                  the same, borderless full screen
    rustyac.exe --windowed                   the endless flat road
    rustyac.exe --track spa --windowed --autodrive
                                             watch a simple driver follow the AI line
    rustyac.exe --car bmw_m3_e30 --track magione --windowed
                                             another car (content\cars\bmw_m3_e30) and track
    rustyac.exe --list-devices               the pads and wheels found, and the bindings
    rustyac.exe --help                       every option

The first run writes rustyac_controls.ini next to rustyac.exe with the bindings
it found (your Assetto Corsa bindings if there are any). That file is yours to
edit; delete it to import the game's bindings again.

The window must have the keyboard focus to drive. Clicking another window
pauses the drive.


3. Keys
-------

Keyboard (yours may differ: the first key is taken from your Assetto Corsa
bindings, the second is added by rustyAC):

    Left / Right, or A / D     steer
    Up, or W                   throttle
    Down, or S                 brake
    Space, or E                gear up
    Left Ctrl, or Q            gear down
    Left Shift                 clutch
    F                          DRS
    F9, or B                   handbrake
    L                          headlights
    Alt + G                    automatic gearbox on / off
    Alt + T                    traction control level up (with Shift: down)
    Alt + A                    ABS level up (with Shift: down)
                               (a Ctrl key that is not a driving key works too;
                               [RUSTYAC] COMMAND_MODIFIER in rustyac_controls.ini
                               chooses: AUTO, ALT, LCTRL or RCTRL)
    C                          next camera: chase, chase far, cockpit
    R                          reset the car to the spawn point
    Shift + R                  back onto the track where the car is
    N                          a brand-new car
    P or Pause                 pause
    Esc                        quit

Xbox pad:

    Left stick                 steer
    RT / LT                    throttle / brake
    Y / X                      gear up / gear down
    A                          clutch
    LB                         DRS
    D-pad right / left         brake bias forward / back
    Right stick press          next camera
    Back (View)                reset the car to the spawn point
    Start (Menu)               pause

A wheel is read through your Assetto Corsa bindings; add --ffb for force
feedback (low strength).
