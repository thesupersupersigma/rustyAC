rustyAC @VERSION@ - how to run
=====================================

rustyAC is a Rust rewrite of Assetto Corsa. It is very experimental: one car on a
track or on an endless flat road. The picture is drawn by a port of the game's
own renderer with the game's own shaders (no post-processing, no mirrors, no
driver yet); --debug-view brings the older, simpler picture back.

Source, notes and newer versions: https://github.com/thesupersupersigma/rustyAC


1. What you need
----------------

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with a graphics card that does Direct3D 11.

- Your own Assetto Corsa install. No Assetto Corsa file comes with rustyAC:
  the tracks, the car models and the car data are read from your own game.
  rustyAC finds the game by itself in Steam's usual place
  (C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\assettocorsa) and in every
  other Steam library (it reads Steam's libraryfolders.vdf). If your game is
  somewhere else, say where it is:

      set AC_ROOT=D:\Games\assettocorsa               (in cmd)
      $env:AC_ROOT = "D:\Games\assettocorsa"          (in PowerShell)

  Nothing has to be unpacked or copied. A car is named by its folder name under
  content\cars (--car ks_ferrari_f2004, which is also the default). Its data is
  read straight out of content\cars\<car>\data.acd, in memory, the way the game
  does it; a car that has a plain "data" folder instead (the SDK's cars,
  unpacked mods) is read from that. Nothing is written into the game's folder.

  --car also takes a folder: a car's folder, or a folder with the plain data
  files (car.ini, tyres.ini, ...) in it:

      rustyac.exe --car "D:\my cars\my_car\data"

  Every car the plain game can load drives. A car made for Custom Shaders
  Patch only (suspension TYPE=COSMIC, or data files encrypted for it) is
  refused with a message that says so.

- Sound is Assetto Corsa's own: rustyAC loads the game's two FMOD DLLs
  (fmod64.dll, fmodstudio64.dll) and its sound banks from your install when it
  starts and plays them the way the game does. None of them comes with rustyAC.
  If the game is not found, rustyAC says so and runs silently.

- Optional: the Visual C++ 2013 runtime (MSVCR120.dll). Assetto Corsa installs
  it, so you most likely have it. With it rustyAC calls the game's own maths
  functions. Without it rustyAC uses its own copy of those functions (sin, cos,
  pow and the like), rewritten in Rust, which gives the same results bit for
  bit on any processor since about 2013; the numbers in the cars' files are
  then read by Rust's own parser, and the sound's level maths is Rust's.
  rustyAC prints which of the two it uses when it starts ("maths: ...").


2. Commands
-----------

Open a console (cmd or PowerShell) in this folder.

    rustyac.exe --track spa --windowed       drive at Spa, in a window
    rustyac.exe --track spa                  the same, borderless full screen
    rustyac.exe --windowed                   the endless flat road
    rustyac.exe --track spa --windowed --autodrive
                                             watch a simple driver follow the AI line
    rustyac.exe --car ks_ferrari_488_gt3 --track magione --windowed
                                             another car (content\cars\ks_ferrari_488_gt3)
                                             and track
    rustyac.exe --track laguna --windowed    a track by a part of its name (Laguna Seca)
    rustyac.exe --track ks_nurburgring --layout layout_sprint_a --windowed
                                             a track with layouts: say which one
    rustyac.exe --list-tracks                every track and layout of your game, and
                                             which ones cannot be driven (and why)
    rustyac.exe --list-devices               the pads and wheels found, and the bindings
    rustyac.exe --help                       every option

The weather is your Assetto Corsa's: air and road temperature, track grip,
wind and the driving aids are read from the session you last set up in the
game (Documents\Assetto Corsa\cfg\race.ini and assists.ini). To change them:

    rustyac.exe --track spa --windowed --air 26 --road 30 --grip 100 --wind 0
    rustyac.exe --track spa --windowed --no-race-ini      26 C / 30 C, full grip, no wind
    rustyac.exe --track spa --windowed --setup mysetup    a setup saved in the game
    rustyac.exe --track spa --windowed --camera car0      start on the car's first camera

The sound's volumes and the output device are your Assetto Corsa's too
(Documents\Assetto Corsa\cfgudio.ini). To change them here:

    rustyac.exe --track spa --windowed --volume 0.4       master volume, 0 to 1
    rustyac.exe --track spa --windowed --no-audio         no sound
    rustyac.exe --track spa --autodrive --headless --duration 40 --audio-wav lap.wav
                                             no window, no speakers: the sound of
                                             a drive into a WAV file

The first run writes rustyac_controls.ini next to rustyac.exe with the bindings
it found (your Assetto Corsa bindings if there are any). That file is yours to
edit; delete it to import the game's bindings again.

The window must have the keyboard focus to drive. Clicking another window
pauses the drive.


3. Keys
-------

Keyboard (yours may differ: the first key is taken from your Assetto Corsa
bindings, the second is added by rustyAC):

    Left / Right, or A / D     steer
    Up, or W                   throttle
    Down, or S                 brake
    Space, or E                gear up
    Left Ctrl, or Q            gear down
    Left Shift                 clutch
    F                          DRS
    F9, or B                   handbrake
    L                          headlights
    Alt + G                    automatic gearbox on / off
    T, or Alt + T              traction control level up (with Shift: down)
    Y, or Alt + A              ABS level up (with Shift: down)
                               (for the Alt commands a Ctrl key that is not a
                               driving key works too; [RUSTYAC] COMMAND_MODIFIER
                               in rustyac_controls.ini chooses: AUTO, ALT, LCTRL
                               or RCTRL)
    ]                          brake bias one click forward
    [                          brake bias one click rearward
                               (T, Y, ] and [ are rustyAC's own keys, the same as
                               in the browser preview. If your bindings use one
                               of them for something else, it keeps that use.
                               ] and [ are the two keys right of P on a US
                               keyboard.)
    F1, or C                   next view: chase, chase 2, bonnet, bumper, dash, cockpit
    F11                        the virtual mirror on or off (needs mirrors in AC's
                               video settings; cockpit and chase views)
    F6                         the car's own cameras (the F2004's first one is above
                               the driver's helmet, looking down the nose); F1 goes back
    R                          reset the car to the spawn point
    Shift + R                  back onto the track where the car is
    N                          a brand-new car
    P or Pause                 pause
    Esc                        quit

Xbox pad:

    Left stick                 steer
    RT / LT                    throttle / brake
    Y / X                      gear up / gear down
    A                          clutch
    LB                         DRS
    D-pad up / down            traction control level up / down
    D-pad right / left         brake bias forward / back
    Back held + D-pad up/down  ABS level up / down
    Right stick press          next view (as F1)
    Back (View), a tap         reset the car to the spawn point
    Back held 0.6 s, let go    back onto the track where the car is
    Start (Menu)               pause

    What Back does is decided when you let it go: nothing if you pressed the
    D-pad's up or down while holding it (it was the ABS combination), else the
    reset after a tap, or "back onto the track" after 0.6 s or more.

The display always shows the levels: "TC 2/3", "ABS off" (or "not fitted" for
a car without it) and "Bias 58.0 %", and a note in the upper middle for a
moment when one of them changes. A level goes up to its highest and then to
off, as in the game. The numbers are the game's own: the level and count its
own message shows, and the brake bias of its shared memory (brakeBias).

A wheel is read through your Assetto Corsa bindings; add --ffb for force
feedback (low strength).
